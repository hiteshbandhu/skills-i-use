# Taste: craft rules for short-form edits

General rules distilled from many iterations. They are defaults, not law; your own preferences
win. Keep a private log of what lands with *your* audience outside this repo (SKILL.md, Step 7)
and update it after every cut.

## The one-line version

**Transform the footage around one clear idea, with bold type and energy.** Subtle, moody or
tech-demo cuts tend to underperform.

## Concept
- One idea per reel, about the subject. Write it in a sentence before building.
- Transform the footage itself (mattes, lifts, type interacting with people), don't just overlay on it.
- Use the footage the person cares about; they notice skipped clips. Include live movement, not only stills.
- "Different" means a new visual language *and* new techniques, not a new grade on the last cut.
- Every effect needs a reason. Effects that only show off a model read as forced.

## Group footage
- Build a story arc. A hero's journey in chapters works well; a string of nice moments is not enough.
- Equal screen time for everyone. Close-ups get equal beats; quotes and titles sit over balanced wide shots or face-free b-roll.
- Never put a panel or type over a face. Place overlays using face boxes (`vision/face`).
- Don't add identity, gender or "agenda" framing the people in the footage didn't choose.
- When someone asks to be removed, remove them from every shot, including wides and other angles.
- Write observations about real people kindly. Get approval before publishing anything about them.

## Type
- Big, few words, punchy. Condensed heavy faces set to fill the width work well.
- No full stops in on-screen headline text.
- Type should interact with the image (inside letters, behind people, at a depth plane). Floating generic captions read as AI-made.

## Image
- Keep it bright. Contrast yes, crushed blacks no.
- Grain, paper, halftone and tape make it feel made by hand, not generated.
- Stop-motion frame rates (12–15 fps) for print and collage looks; 30 fps for camera moves.

## Motion and sync
- Cut on beats; drops land on kicks; a 3-frame punch-in on hard cuts.
- Measure every motion target (matte, face, hand or pose JSON, DOM rect) and verify it on a zoomed frame.

## Captions
- Phrase-sized chunks with keyword emphasis, inside the safe zone (max width ~860 px, baseline above y 1500).

## Sound
- Real music plus restrained SFX tied to visible events.
- Soft audio edges: 30–60 ms fades, room tone under jump cuts, J/L cuts.
- Master to -14 LUFS with a static gain plus a limiter.

## Process
- Ask before downloading outside assets; state the source and licence. Flag rights risks (commercial songs, game audio).
- Flag any fact you add that the speaker did not say.
- Machine-translated subtitles must be checked by a speaker of the language.
- Never publish personal details about the people in the footage, the client or the project in shared or public files.
